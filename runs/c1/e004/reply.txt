The diagnostics put the error in the class decision (wrong-class cost ≈0.117 mAP, dominated by deposit↔stain and pore→stain), and weight averaging is a variance-reducer that sharpens the decision boundary on exactly those hard, visually-similar pairs without changing what the model sees or its ranking rule. It is fundamentally different from the tried "ensembles" (multiple independently trained models) and "longer training" (more steps): EMA re-uses the single existing trajectory at near-zero compute cost, so it fits the budget and should add a small but broad, multi-class gain rather than a one-class bump.

HYPOTHESIS: Replace the fixed ImageNet global normalization with per-crop, per-channel normalization (subtract the crop's own mean, divide by its own std). Industrial weld images almost certainly have varying illumination across and within images; with global normalization, the same defect type under brighter vs. darker lighting produces very different input tensors, and the model may confuse brightness-driven differences with texture differences (e.g. a bright stain vs. a dark deposit). Per-crop normalization makes the classifier illumination-invariant, targeting the deposit↔stain (101 cases) and pore→stain (20 cases) confusions across all classes. This is a data-pipeline change distinct from every previously tried family (resolution, backbone, loss, augmentation, scoring, extra channels).

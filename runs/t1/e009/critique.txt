VERDICT: APPROVE  
REASONS:
- The idea of augmenting training with detector proposals has been tried before, but only with a static fusion. Combining it with the new learnable per‑class fusion is a fundamentally different mechanism and is likely to yield a broader gain across classes.
- Adding detector boxes only increases the dataset size; earlier similar experiments ran within the 180 s budget, so the time impact is manageable.
- The implementation requires only modest changes (compute IoU, extend the crop list), which are unlikely to introduce hard bugs.
- Expected impact is across multiple defect types (especially pore and deposit) and not limited to a single image or class.

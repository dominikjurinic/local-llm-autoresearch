HYPOTHESIS: The dominant error is wrong class assignment on detector boxes (wrong-class loss ≈ +0.11 mAP). The current model trains only on crops.csv ground-truth boxes, but at test time it classifies dets.csv detector boxes whose size/position distribution differs from perfect GT. The untried combination here is to train the e008 learnable-tight/wide-fusion classifier on BOTH the ground-truth crops and the detector proposals labelled by IoU>0.5 with their true class, so the classifier sees exactly the inputs it will judge. This should lift the weak classes (pore 62%, deposit 77%) substantially, whereas earlier runs of this idea alone were marginal and uncombined with per-class adaptive fusion.

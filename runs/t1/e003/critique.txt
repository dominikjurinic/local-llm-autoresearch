VERDICT: REVISE  
REASONS:  
- Adding detector boxes as training examples was already explored (6 attempts) with only ~+0.004 gains; the new proposal merely oversamples the same signal and is unlikely to yield a >0.005 effect.  
- The change would enlarge the training set considerably, increasing epoch time and risking hitting the 180 s wall‑clock limit.  
- The proposed hard‑negative strategy drops all proposals that fail IoU ≥ 0.5, so it adds no new negative examples; it does not address the key confusion between deposit/stain or pore.  
- It may introduce duplicate boxes (same crop appears as GT and proposal) causing noisy gradients.  
- The expected benefit is narrow (mainly deposit‑vs‑stain) and may not generalise across all classes or images.  

**Suggestion**: instead, try a different approach that targets the class confusion directly, e.g.  
- Train the classifier on the *full* two‑view crops (tight + wide) with a *single* resolution (e.g. 224 px) and apply a *temperature‑scaled* softmax to improve ranking without changing the class distribution.  
- Or incorporate a *multi‑scale* (e.g. 128 px + 224 px) training to give the network richer texture cues, while keeping the number of views per example minimal to stay within the time budget.  
- Alternatively, use a lightweight *ensemble* of two models trained with different seeds and average their logits; this has proven effective for ranking metrics under a strict compute budget.

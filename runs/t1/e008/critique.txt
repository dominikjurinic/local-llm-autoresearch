VERDICT: REVISE  
REASONS:  
- The proposal falls under the “Resolution and small‑box handling” family, which already had 9 attempts with no confirmed gain.  
- Raising only the tight crop resolution is unlikely to produce a broad improvement; it targets only the small pores/deposits and may not shift the overall ranking.  
- Doubling the tight‑crop size will increase inference time for every eval box and may push the run past the 180 s limit, especially when paired with the wide view.  
- The code changes are fragile: the new `size` argument must propagate through the dataset, loader, and training loop, and a mismatch can silently corrupt the view ordering or batch shape.  
- A more promising direction is to try a different backbone that has not yet been explored (e.g., `convnext_nano`, `mobilenetv3_large_100`, or `regnety_016`) while keeping the current two‑crop resolution scheme, or to experiment with a learned fusion weight between the tight and wide logits.  This would stay within the budget and offers a clearer, broader potential benefit.

HYPOTHESIS: The detector's boxes for small defects are weak (IoU < 0.5 or false alarms), and our stage-2 classifier cannot recover them because every eval box is scored as if it were a real defect. Re-ranking each class's rows with an IoU-quality signal would demote the many false alarms that currently sit at the top of the ranking. The gap to the perfect-class reference (0.6288 → 0.7461) is dominated by wrong classes on overlapping boxes, but a second-order gain comes from letting our rows compete fairly against the detector's own ranking.
PLAN: Compute, for every eval box, a per-class quality estimate: (a) detector conf d["conf"], and (b) an IoU-based prior from the tight-crop classifier by looking at overlap of that box with *all* training crops in crops.csv — but we cannot use test labels. Instead, use the detector's own class-agnostic confidence as a proxy for box quality across classes: score[c] = conf * p[c]^gamma with gamma < 1 to reduce the dominance of high-confidence false alarms. Actually, the strongest cheap change is to rank per-class rows by a monotone function that preserves order — this cannot help. Therefore: replace score by conf * p[c]^2 for all classes. This keeps the model's class discrimination but down-weights the classifier's confidence where it disagrees with the detector, reordering rows only within a class between high and low confusion. Expected gain ~0.003-0.008, no extra compute.

This targets the same per-class accuracy gap but with a fundamentally different model family than the EfficientNet experiments. Note: as written, convnext_nano at 160px with two views would blow the 55s prediction reserve (~2.5x the EfficientNet-B0 prediction time), so I keep the idea but use convnext_pico (a ConvNeXt backbone, ~9M params, still more capacity than EfficientNet-B0) which fits the budget with margin; LR and weight decay are tuned for stable ConvNeXt fine-tuning as specified.

HYPOTHESIS: The dominant error is wrong class assignment on overlapping boxes (~0.11 mAP), concentrated in deposit↔stain (97 misclassified) and pore→stain. Training a classifier only on ground-truth crops never sees the exact input distribution at test time, where detector boxes are smaller and noisier. Retraining the same e008 architecture on a *mixture* of GT crops and in-situ detector-box crops (the latter giving an extra +0.004 mAP already) should directly repair that loss by teaching the model the geometry/texture statistics of detector boxes, which is where most confusions are made.
PLAN: 1) In `main()`, after reading `crops` and `dets`, build a second list of training rows from dets.csv "train" split boxes: for each det with IoU>0.5 against a crops.csv box on the same image, take that crop row but overwrite `cls` with the overlap box's class. 2) Merge this list into the training set (keep the GT `crops` rows too). 3) Compute IoU in code: `iou = (x1o*x2o & intersect area) / union`. 4) Keep everything else identical to e008 (two crops, per-class fusion), so step time is unchanged. The added training rows are cheap (no extra model passes); total seconds should stay ~162s within the 180s budget.

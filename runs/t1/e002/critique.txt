VERDICT: REVISE  
REASONS:
- The proposal adds a second high‑resolution branch, turning the two‑crop model into a multi‑resolution one.  
- This idea family (extra views, resolution changes) has been tested before; adding a third view yielded only ~0.005 gain and pushed the run close to the time limit.  
- The added branch will roughly 1.5× increase per‑forward cost; with two passes per eval box the prediction time will likely exceed the 180 s budget even after lowering RESERVE_S.  
- Implementing two crops of different sizes requires non‑trivial changes to `make_crop`, dataset handling, and the training loop, increasing the risk of a bug.  
- The expected benefit (small‑class improvements) may be limited and not broad enough to be statistically significant.  

Suggested next step: keep the original two‑crop design but replace the 2.5× 160 px crop with a **single** higher‑resolution crop (e.g., 2.5× resized to 224 px) while still using the 1.5× 160 px tight crop. This keeps the number of forward passes the same as the baseline, preserves the budget, and still tests the hypothesis that higher resolution on the wide context helps. If this yields a noticeable gain, later experiments can explore a fully multi‑resolution (e.g., 160 px + 224 px) two‑branch architecture.

HYPOTHESIS: The two crops (tight 1.5x + wide 2.5x) at 160px already give good context, but the model never sees the box edges at a resolution high enough to separate fine texture of deposit/stain and pore/stain. Making the tight crop dominant and running the whole classification stack at higher effective resolution (a second branch on 224px crops of the wide view) should recover the per-class accuracy that a perfect-box classifier reaches (macro-F1 0.87). The expected gain is ~0.01-0.02 mAP on the weak pore and deposit classes, unlike earlier resolution attempts which only changed IMG_SIZE or MIN_CROP without adding resolution to the key view.
PLAN:
1. Keep the two-crop structure but make the tight crop (1.5x) the "primary" view and keep the wide view (2.5x) as context. Add a third branch: the wide view cropped at the original box size but resized to 224px instead of 160px, giving higher effective resolution for fine texture.
2. Replace the simple logit average with a weighted average where the tight-branch and the high-res wide branch are averaged and then softmaxed; this uses shared backbone features at two resolutions.
3. Adjust RESERVE_S downward from 55 to 40 because prediction now runs three branches instead of two; check step budget stays within the 180s total (each additional branch adds ~20% forward-time per eval box).
4. Keep IMG_SIZE=160 for the tight branch (retains original behavior), use 224px for the new wide high-res branch, and run all three through the same EfficientNet-B0.

HYPOTHESIS: The score is `conf * p_c`, but the closed-set classifier is confident even on false alarms and on misassigned true boxes, so `p_c` is a noisy ranking signal that can demote true boxes (low p_c) below confident FPs/misassignments (high p_c) within a class, which is why we sit at 0.6288 while the conf-scored ceiling is 0.758. I replace the product with `conf * (p_c/mean_c)^0.3`, i.e. let the class-agnostic detector conf dominate the order (as in the ceiling) while keeping a mild, mean-normalized class-likelihood term so the four classes stay separated. This is pure re-scoring of the same trained model (no retrain), directly targets the within-class ordering that mAP@0.5 depends on, and differs from all prior runs, which changed training data/architecture/resolution, not the score.

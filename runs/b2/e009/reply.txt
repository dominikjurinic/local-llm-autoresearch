Stain vs deposit and pore vs background likely depend on the extent of the discoloration/material relative to the surrounding bead, which a 2.5x crop mostly misses; the paper's one-step detector (0.638, above us) sees full-image context, which is the one signal we lack. This differs from e003 (which raised pixel resolution of the same views) and e002 (more parameters) — it adds a new scale of context at zero architectural cost, and the extra forward pass fits the budget (predicted ~178s total).

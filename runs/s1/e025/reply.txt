HYPOTHESIS: The dominant 0.117 mAP loss is wrong class assignment (pore↔stain, deposit↔stain), and it comes from a model that sees every defect at only one scale. Pores are tiny so a 160px tight crop often shows just a dark blob that looks like a stain; stains are diffuse so the wide 2.5x crop dilutes their texture. Running *both* crops at the backbone's native 224 px (EfficientNet-B0 is trained at 224, we currently resize to 160) keeps fine pore edges sharp and lets the wide context resolve stains — a fix that has never been tested cleanly (e002/e003 crashed with 224px because batch size wasn't halved and no channels-last; this version halves the batch and keeps the reserve). Expected gain ~+0.01 mAP if resolution is the pore/stain bottleneck.

HYPOTHESIS: Replace the logit-averaging of two independent EfficientNet-B0 forward passes with a shared backbone whose 1280-dim pooled features from the tight and wide crops are concatenated (2560-dim) before a small two-layer head. Averaging 4-dim logits forces each view to be classified independently and discards complementary information; concatenating features lets the head learn how to weigh fine texture (tight view, critical for pore) against surrounding context (wide view, critical for stain) jointly. This should reduce the pore→stain (20) and deposit→stain (55) confusions that dominate the 0.117 mAP gap between our score and the perfect-class ceiling, and should move all four classes. Unlike the rejected box-jitter (e003) and detector-box (e002) experiments, this changes how the model combines its two views rather than what boxes or jitter it is trained on, and costs zero extra forward passes.

class TwoViewClassifier(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, backbone, head):
        super().__init__()
        self.backbone = backbone
        self.head = head

    def forward(self, x1, x2):
        f1 = self.backbone(x1)
        f2 = self.backbone(x2)
        return self.head(torch.cat([f1, f2], dim=1))

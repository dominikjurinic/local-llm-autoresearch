For a detection to be a TP:

2. It must be the correct class
3. It must be ranked above other detections

The "ranking" problem: a true detection has a lower score than a false alarm. This hurts AP.

- conf is the detector's confidence (moderate to high)
- p[c] is the classifier's probability for the correct class (moderate to high)

For a false alarm:
- conf is the detector's confidence (low to moderate)
- p[c] is the classifier's probability for some class (could be anything)

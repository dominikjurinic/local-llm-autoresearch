HYPOTHESIS: Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) averages model weights along the training trajectory (typically the last 30% of training), improving generalization and calibration with negligible compute overhead. Unlike the EMA attempts (e014, e015) which crashed due to CPU-GPU transfers, SWA can be implemented efficiently on-device by maintaining a shadow model updated in-place. This is a standard technique orthogonal to architecture/data changes that could provide the broad, cross-class improvement needed for confirmation. Expected gain: 0.005-0.01 mAP.

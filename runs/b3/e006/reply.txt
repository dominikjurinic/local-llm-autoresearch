HYPOTHESIS: e004 showed that training on detector boxes matched to true boxes (IoU>0.5) helps, and the 0.634 vs 0.758 ceiling gap is dominated by the classifier failing on exactly the boxes the detector emits (misplaced, partially outside the defect). Loosening the match to IoU>0.3 adds the near-miss detector boxes - crops that are a mix of defect and adjacent background - which are the regime the model never sees in ground-truth-box training, and should sharpen pore/deposit/stain separation on real inference boxes. Expected gain ~0.01-0.02; it differs from e004 (same idea, wider threshold, strictly more data) and from e002 (resolution, not box placement), and costs no inference time.

Two deviations from the plan, both forced by the measured time budget (load 13s, prediction ~73s):
1. Kept RESERVE_S=85 instead of 60 — with 73s of prediction, a 60s reserve would push the run past 180s and time out. I instead cap initial training at 45% of the training budget, run the forward-only cleaning pass (~10s, since it classifies 6052 boxes vs 41879 for prediction), then retrain for the rest.
2. SWA snapshots are collected every 10 steps during the retrain phase (not fixed global steps 20/40/60/80, which would land in the wrong phase) and averaged with a dtype-safe `swa_average` that skips non-float tensors (the exact e021 crash). If the cleaning pass overruns, the retrain is skipped (guarded by `time.time() < t_end - 5`) so the run still finishes.

HYPOTHESIS: The classifier is trained only on ground-truth boxes but must classify stage-1 detector boxes at test time — a distribution mismatch (offset, size) that hits the weak classes hardest. Training on the actual detector boxes from the train split, labelled by overlap with GT, matches the test distribution and should raise pore/deposit accuracy; this combines the winning 4-scale pyramid with the only previously confirmed data-side gain (+0.004 mAP in the two-crop runs).
PLAN: 1) Read dets.csv, take boxes with split == "train", and find those overlapping a GT box (from crops.csv on high-res images) by IoU > 0.5; keep the GT class label, dropping any box that overlaps no GT box. 2) Concatenate these detector-box rows to the crop rows for training (the eval boxes stay in the same loader structure but are only used for inference). 3) Pass a flag into BoxDataset so train-time crops use the same make_crop but the extra rows need no image lookups beyond what is already loaded. 4) Estimate time: detector-box count roughly doubles the training set; keep steps similar by dropping the earliest batches or reducing epoch loops, and verify total runtime stays under ~160s with RESERVE_S=85 before submitting.

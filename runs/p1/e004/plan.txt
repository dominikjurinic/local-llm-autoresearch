The key insight: this isn't a resolution/throughput problem — it's a calibration/bias problem. A per-class adjustment of the classifier probabilities directly re-orders which class wins inside each box, fixing exactly these wrong-class mistakes. Earlier score-row manipulation (argmax, margin ranking) didn't work because they operate on the product with detector conf; here I change only the class probability scale within a box, leaving cross-box ordering alone.
